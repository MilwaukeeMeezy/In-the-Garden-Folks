How it works

  1. 1

    Add your space

    Pick your city, point the compass and outline your yard, patio or plot. No account needed to try it.

  2. 2

    Explore sun, soil & water

    Drag the sun across the day, log soil tests and mark where water pools after rain.

  3. 3

    Plan and grow

    Match native and edible plants to each bed, then follow seasonal tasks and keep a journal.
